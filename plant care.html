Succulent Care

1.Water sparingly: Let the soil dry out completely before watering again.
2.Sunlight: Keep in bright, indirect sunlight; avoid direct sun to prevent burns.

Fern Care

1.Water regularly: Keep the soil consistently moist, but ensure good drainage to prevent root rot.
2.Light: Place in indirect light; avoid direct sunlight to prevent leaf burn.

Snake Plant Care

1. Place snake plants in bright, indirect light and water sparingly, allowing the soil to dry out between waterings.
2. Avoid overwatering and ensure the pot has good drainage.

Cactus Care

1. Cacti need bright, direct sunlight and should be watered thoroughly but infrequently, allowing the soil to dry completely between waterings.
2.Ensure they’re in well-draining soil and pots with drainage holes.

Peace Lily Care

1. Peace lilies thrive in medium to low light and prefer consistently moist soil, but avoid waterlogging.
2.Keep them in a humid environment and wipe their leaves regularly to remove dust.

Ficus Care

1.Ficus plants prefer bright, indirect light and should be watered when the top inch of soil feels dry.
2.Avoid sudden temperature changes and keep them away from drafts.

Alovera care

1.Aloe vera needs bright, indirect sunlight and should be watered sparingly, allowing the soil to dry completely between waterings.
2.Use well-draining soil and avoid overwatering to prevent root rot.

Pothos care

1.Pothos thrive in low to bright indirect light and prefer to dry out between waterings.
2.They tolerate neglect, so avoid overwatering and ensure proper drainage.

Spider plant care

1.Spider plants prefer bright, indirect light and should be watered when the soil is dry to the touch.
2.Avoid letting them sit in water to prevent root rot. /p>

Cast Iron Plant (Aspidistra elatior)

1.Cast Iron plants prefer low to moderate light and should be watered when the top inch of soil is dry.
2.They’re drought-tolerant, so avoid overwatering and ensure proper drainage.

Chinese Evergreen (Aglaonema)

1.Chinese Evergreens thrive in low to moderate light and prefer to be kept slightly moist, with the soil drying out between waterings.
2.Keep them in warm, humid conditions and avoid cold drafts.

Fiddle Leaf Fig (Ficus lyrata)

1.Fiddle Leaf Figs thrive in bright, indirect light and prefer consistently moist, well-draining soil.
2.Avoid overwatering and ensure good drainage to prevent root rot.

Bamboo Palm (Chamaedorea seifrizii)

1.Bamboo Palms thrive in moderate, indirect light and prefer consistently moist, well-draining soil.
2.Keep humidity high and avoid direct sunlight to prevent leaf burn.

Pilea (Pilea peperomioides)

1.Pilea prefers bright, indirect light and well-draining soil that’s kept slightly moist.
2.Allow the top inch of soil to dry out between waterings to prevent root rot.

String of Pearls

1.String of Pearls thrives in bright, direct sunlight and well-draining soil.
2.Water sparingly, allowing the soil to dry out completely between waterings to prevent root rot.
